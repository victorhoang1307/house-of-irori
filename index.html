DISCOVER THE STORY

HIDDEN IN PLAIN SIGHT

House of IRORI isn't a cocktail bar. It's a moment - a small corner where time moves differently. Step down a narrow alley.

Warm tungsten light spills across dark wood. A bartender works with quiet precision. Ice hits glass. Smoke rises slowly. This is omotenashi. Japanese hospitality in its purest form. Every detail whispered, never shouted.

THE SPACE BETWEEN,
STILLNESS AND BEAUTY

TWO FLOORS. ONE PHILOSOPHY.

LEVEL ONE:

THE ALLEY

A cinematic passage. Narrow. Intimate. Like stepping into a 70s Tokyo film. Dark wood weathered by time. Warm lighting that doesn't reveal everything. Walls that feel like they have stories. This is where you arrive.

LEVEL TWO:

THE BAR

The heart of the experience. A small counter where the bartender becomes the story. Watch spirit and ice transform into something sacred. Every pour is a conversation.

This is where you stay.
